About the Books

Not on My Bus

Not on My Bus is a memoir detailing Queen Saba Danika's journey of faith, personal growth, and resilience. It explores her transition from Catholicism to Christianity, challenges she faced, and lessons she learned along the way. Are we living in Revelation?

I never in my wildest imagination thought I would be jailed for loving God.

After coming to Christ and converting from Catholicism to Christianity, the author's entire life changed! Based on a true story. This book will demonstrate her love for God, His Glory, and the many times His angels took charge over her as it is written and saved her life. After experiencing many life altering tragedies, the mystery of her birth was revealed. This book will demonstrate the author's love for God, His promises when we seek Him and the corruption that is attempting to take God's children in America and around the world. Can you solve the mysteries?
